Browser Privacy

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026. How the NoorSoul static website handles browser storage without invasive ad trackers.

1. No Advertising or Tracking Cookies

The NoorSoul website (`noor-app.com`) is a fast static site that does not set third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or cross-site behavioural trackers.

2. Essential Local Browser Storage

When you enter your email in the app download modal to request a store link, your browser may store a local flag (`noorsoul_waitlist_v1`) in `localStorage` purely to confirm your submission state on your own device.

3. Typography & External Assets

Fonts (`Amiri`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`) are requested from Google Fonts so Arabic calligraphy and Latin headings render properly across devices.

4. Managing Browser Storage

You can clear `localStorage` or block third-party requests at any time through your browser's privacy settings. If you have questions, write to Contact@noorsoul.co.uk.
