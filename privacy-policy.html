1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Noor (NoorSoul) mobile applications (iOS and Android) and the official website at noor-app.com, operated by NoorSoul (Founder: Faisal Abdul Mateen Wasif).

2. Information We Collect

Account & Authentication Data

When you sign up in the Noor mobile app using email/password or Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address, unique user ID, and basic authentication tokens so your profile stays synced across sessions.

Spiritual Profile & Practice Inputs

  • Name & Abjad Profile: The Arabic or English name you enter to compute your Abjad total, element, Divine Name, Wafq constant, and crystal recommendation.
  • Activity & Trackers: Saved Compatibility readings, Wazifa tracker daily progress, Muraqaba depth logs, Ruqyah session logs, and saved Nuqush artworks.
  • Alim AI Conversations: Questions you submit to Alim AI along with your profile context so the assistant can provide personalised Islamic scholarly explanations.

Subscription & Diagnostics Data

  • Purchases: Handled directly by Apple App Store and Google Play through RevenueCat. We receive entitlement status (Free vs. NoorSoul+) and never access or store your credit/debit card details.
  • Stability & Analytics: Anonymous crash stack traces (Firebase Crashlytics) and basic feature usage metrics (Firebase Analytics) to fix bugs and improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To compute and store your personal Abjad profile, daily wird, and Hijri-aligned dhikr reminders.
  • To send optional local or push prayer-time notifications (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) when enabled by you.
  • To process Alim AI queries securely via backend Firebase Cloud Functions using the Anthropic Claude API without exposing API keys or selling prompt history.
  • To facilitate confidential 1-on-1 session bookings inside Murshid Connect.

Zero Ad-Tracking Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or share your personal data or spiritual logs with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. User records in Google Firebase Firestore are protected by strict user-scoped security rules so only you can access your personal spiritual profile and history. We retain your account data only while your account remains active or as required by law.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, export, correct, or permanently delete your account and all cloud-stored spiritual history at any time. To request complete account deletion or exercise GDPR/UK-GDPR data rights, email wasifiah@gmail.com from your registered email address.

6. Children's Privacy

Noor is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country) without parental supervision, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
