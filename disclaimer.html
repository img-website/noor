1. No Divination, Astrology or Magic

In Islamic theology, knowledge of the unseen (Ilm al-Ghayb) belongs to Allah alone. Noor's Abjad Profile Engine (Hisab al-Jummal), Compatibility readings, and Nuqush Studio (Ilm al-Wafq) are built strictly for Tafakkur (contemplation), historical Islamic education, and structured remembrance (dhikr).

They do not predict the future, cast spells, or replace reliance upon Allah (Tawakkul) and obligatory Islamic worship.

2. Classical Crystals (Ilm al-Jawahir)

Crystals and gemstones featured in Noor are cited from classical Islamic medical and mineralogical manuscripts — notably Ibn Sina's Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb and Al-Biruni's Kitab al-Jamahir. Crystals hold no independent power; they are presented purely as natural creations of Allah that may accompany personal intention and reflection.

3. Not Medical, Psychological or Psychiatric Advice

Ruqyah recitations, Muraqaba mindfulness, and historical crystal benefits inside Noor are spiritual wellness tools and are not a substitute for licensed medical diagnosis, prescription medication, or emergency mental-health care. Always consult a qualified doctor for physical or psychological conditions.

4. Not a Fatwa / Scholarly Supervision

Alim AI provides educational summaries rooted in our classical Islamic knowledge corpus and does not issue formal legal verdicts (Fatwa). High-caution traditional practices are marked with caution levels and explicitly directed toward qualified scholars via Murshid Connect.
